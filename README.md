it's a game where the gameplay loop involves managing a nuclear facility, at first it is manual, but as time goes on you gain opportunities to make it automated. There will be a manual left there along with a single hard drive that contains  a very simple os and limited space on it. This is basically level 1 of automation, you have to automate the reactor using a simple programming language (there will be some super simple not optimized scripts on the computer already, as well as other hard drives you can find and recover throughout the game that may contain slightly better variations or different os's).

Essentially the player discovers after they first begin playing that their father has just died and now they are responsible for maintaining the nuclear facility that has been left to his family as part of a post apocalyptic contingency plan. The family was to basically reproduce for generations and maintain the facility as long as possible until society manages to recover eneough to pick up where it left off.

I plan on there being snippets of lore in recovered hard drives and such, as well as lore within the family via notes from your late father/mother or whatever.

As time goes on the community around your reactor starts to give you "Scrap" that they found outside in exchange for the power which you can then sort through and find either knick knacks or stuff that you can  recover (electronics of various disrepair) for use in your facility.
